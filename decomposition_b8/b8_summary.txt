------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION / ORDER-DEPENDENCE (Step 5)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The prevalence x residual interaction is nonzero by construction (the noise
floor depends on class mix). The Shapley value -- averaging both orderings --
absorbs this interaction symmetrically into both components. The per-fold
'order_swing_relative_to_total_gap' column in the detail CSV is exactly the
magnitude of what a purely additive, single-ordering ledger would have
misattributed entirely to whichever component was computed first.
