VERDICT (Issue B)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The figure is right and the docstring is wrong. Under the saved data the real
  STRING graph sits BELOW the empty graph on AUC at every masking rate, and falls
  faster, so its AUC flatness advantage is negative with a CI excluding zero.

  The direction does NOT depend on the aggregation. Sample-pooled and
  mean-of-per-cohort agree in sign at rate 0; only the level shifts.

  The direction DOES depend on the METRIC, and this is a real result, not an
  artefact:
    auc           flatness advantage -0.0077 [-0.0100,-0.0056] -> FAVOURS the no-graph control
    macro_f1      flatness advantage +0.0105 [+0.0006,+0.0205] -> FAVOURS the graph
    balanced_acc  flatness advantage +0.0094 [+0.0017,+0.0171] -> FAVOURS the graph
  Ranking (AUC) degrades faster with the graph; thresholded decision quality
  (macro-F1, balanced accuracy) degrades more slowly with it. Both are measured on
  identical gene subsets, paired at the draw level, so this is not sampling noise:
  the graph is stabilising the decision boundary while not helping the ranking.

  Reported at face value: there is NO single-number 'robustness advantage'. Any
  claim of one must name its metric. The regenerated fig11 shows both metrics and
  the signed advantage side by side so the disagreement is visible, not chosen.
